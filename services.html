Our Work

How We deliver

Three ways WVTR gets clean water to families — repairing what's broken, reaching new water, and treating water at the source.

Well Repair

Well Repair

Many communities already have a well — it's just broken. Repairing an existing well is one of the fastest, most cost-effective ways to restore safe water to an entire village. WVTR funds parts, labor, and local training so repairs last.

Photo: Drilling a new well
Drilling

Drilling new wells

Where there's no nearby source, we work with local drilling partners to reach clean groundwater — bringing safe water within walking distance and cutting the daily hours families spend hauling water.

Fund Drilling
Filters

Filters at the source

Sawyer filtration systems make water safe to drink right where it's collected — removing bacteria and protozoa that cause disease. Filters are affordable, durable, and easy for families to use and maintain for years.
