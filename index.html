01 The Water Crisis

Water Without Limits

Water affects health, hygiene, education, safety, opportunity, and time. When clean water becomes accessible, everything around it begins to change.

/01

The Walk

Families carry water for miles because access is not close enough — time that could go to school, work, and family.

/02

200 Million Hours

Every day, communities lose hundreds of millions of hours collecting water — hours that nearby access gives back.

/03

Bacteria & Disease

Contaminated water spreads illness that keeps children out of school and families out of work. Clean water breaks the cycle.

/04

Access

Clean water should be close, safe, and reliable. Reliable access is the foundation everything else is built on.

03 Flagship Project

Project Gowba

Project Gowba is WVTR's on-the-ground commitment to a community that needs reliable water most. It combines filters, education, and infrastructure so clean water stays close, safe, and dependable long after the team leaves.

Field work means: filters, education, community support, and clean water access — delivered directly, and built to last.

04 Our Story

Not centralized

WVTR was built on a simple belief: clean water should be close, safe, and reliable for everyone. We work through real-world projects and humanitarian partnerships, going directly to the communities that need water access most.

As a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, every project is transparent, hands-on, and designed for lasting impact — not a one-time fix, but sustainable access that keeps giving time, health, and opportunity back to families.

06 Donate

Give Water. give life

Your gift funds filters, well repairs, storage tanks, and field operations — going directly to the communities that need it.

Where Your Gift Goes

  • Filters — Sawyer filtration systems that make water safe to drink at the source.
  • Well repairs — restoring broken water sources so whole communities regain access.
  • Storage tanks — reliable storage that keeps clean water available year-round.
  • Field operations — boots-on-the-ground work, education, and community support.

WVTR is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit — donations may be tax-deductible.
